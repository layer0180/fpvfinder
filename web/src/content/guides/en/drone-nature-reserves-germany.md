Much of Germany's finest countryside is protected – but not every protected
area is closed to drones. What counts is **the kind** of area. In short:

| Area | Hobby FPV |
|---|---|
| National park (Nationalpark) | **banned** (unless the nature conservation authority consents or state law says otherwise) |
| Nature reserve (Naturschutzgebiet) | **banned** (likewise) |
| Natura 2000: FFH and bird protection areas | **banned** (likewise) |
| Landscape protection area (Landschaftsschutzgebiet) | not banned across the board – **the area's ordinance decides** |
| Nature park, biosphere reserve (outside the core zone) | not banned across the board; parts are often nature or landscape protection areas |

> As of October 2026. Not legal advice. State law can differ – check every spot
> on [dipul.de](https://www.dipul.de/homepage/en/).

## Nature reserves, national parks, Natura 2000

§ 21h (3) no. 6 of the German Air Traffic Order allows operation over nature
reserves, national parks and Natura 2000 sites (FFH and bird protection areas)
only in exceptional cases:

- when the **competent nature conservation authority has expressly consented**,
- when **state law** regulates drone operation there differently,
- or – not in national parks – when **all** of the following hold: not a sport
  or leisure flight, above 100 m, the remote pilot knows and respects the
  purpose of protection, and flying over the area is unavoidable for the
  purpose of the flight.

The third option is out for hobby FPV, since it expressly excludes leisure
flights. **In practice: you do not fly over these areas.** And it is about the
airspace *above* the area – taking off outside and flying in is just as much a
breach.

## Landscape protection areas

Landscape protection areas are **not** listed in § 21h. That does not mean
anything goes: each one has its own **ordinance**, and some expressly ban model
flying, launching aircraft or noise. You usually find the ordinance on the
district's website or the environmental portal of your state.

Landscape protection areas are often huge and cover whole stretches of land
around cities, so many good spots lie inside them – the
[FPV Spot Finder](/en/app) flags them with a note rather than blocking them.

## Nature parks and biosphere reserves

A **nature park** is mainly a recreation and tourism label, not a flight ban.
Nature parks do contain nature and landscape protection areas, for which the
rules above apply. In **biosphere reserves**, the core zones are usually nature
reserves.

## Outside, too: don't disturb wildlife

The Federal Nature Conservation Act bans **significantly disturbing** wild
animals of strictly protected species and European birds during breeding,
rearing, hibernation and migration – inside a protected area or not. A quad
circling low over a lapwing's nest or a heron colony is exactly that. When
birds take off or call in alarm: back off or fly elsewhere.

## How do I recognise a protected area?

- **dipul map:** shows nature reserves, national parks, FFH and bird
  protection areas as geozones. Landscape protection areas are not geozones
  under § 21h – for them only the ordinance helps.
- **Signs on site:** nature and landscape protection areas are usually signed
  along paths and boundaries.
- **FPV Spot Finder:** points in nature reserves, national parks and Natura
  2000 sites are blocked; landscape protection areas get a warning. Under
  "Expert" you can include nature and bird protection areas, for instance if
  you hold a permit – such spots then always carry a red note naming the kind
  of area. The data
  comes from OpenStreetMap and is not complete – dipul is authoritative.

## Frequently asked questions

**May I fly at the edge of a nature reserve?** Yes, as long as you do not fly
over it. Keep a safety margin – an FPV quad crosses the boundary quickly.

**Does the ban apply to drones under 250 g?** Yes. § 21h (3) no. 6 makes no
exception for weight.

**Can I apply for an exemption?** With the competent lower nature conservation
authority, yes – but it is rarely granted for leisure flights.
