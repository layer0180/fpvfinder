**Geographical zones** are areas where drones may only fly under certain
conditions – around airports, over nature reserves, along railway lines and in
many other places. They are defined in **§ 21h of the German Air Traffic Order
(LuftVO)** and drawn on the free **dipul map** of the federal government. A look
at that map belongs before every first flight at a new spot.

> As of October 2026. Not legal advice. The dipul map shows the zones; reading
> their conditions is still up to you.

## Step by step

1. **Open the map:** [maptool-dipul.dfs.de](https://maptool-dipul.dfs.de/).
2. **Go to your spot:** search for the place or zoom in. In the FPV Spot Finder
   you can copy a spot's coordinates with one tap and paste them there.
3. **Check the layers:** coloured areas are zones. Tap an area to see which
   zone it is.
4. **Read the condition:** not every zone is a ban – many require consent, a
   distance or a certain height. What matters is what applies to **your**
   flight: hobby, open category, with a camera.
5. **Think beyond the take-off point:** a 5-inch quad is 300 metres away in
   seconds. Check the whole area you intend to fly in.

## The zones that matter most for FPV

**Nature reserves, national parks, Natura 2000 sites.** Practically off limits
for sport and leisure flights. Only with consent of the nature conservation
authority, or where state law says otherwise.

**Residential properties.** Overflight only with consent of the owner or user.
The exception for light drones applies only without camera and without radio
transmission – an FPV drone never qualifies.

**Federal highways, federal waterways, railway lines.** 100 m lateral distance.
Closer only if your height always stays below the lateral distance and that
distance exceeds 10 m – or with the operator's consent.

**Airfields and airports.** 1.5 km around airfields; 1 km around airports plus
5 km along the extended runways. Control zones only with clearance from air
traffic control.

**Industrial sites, hospitals, authorities, accident and emergency scenes.**
100 m distance, otherwise consent required.

**Outdoor pools and bathing beaches.** Only outside bathing and opening hours.

## What the dipul map does not show

- **Whether people are there.** The map knows nothing about walkers, cyclists or
  a barbecue on the meadow. Distances to uninvolved people are yours to keep on
  site.
- **The 150-metre rule of subcategory A3.** It applies to residential,
  commercial, industrial and recreational areas – regardless of zones.
- **Who owns the meadow.** Taking off and landing on private land needs the
  owner's permission.

## How the finder and dipul work together

The [FPV Spot Finder](/en/app) searches your area for places with few people and
open ground, and already blocks points in nature reserves, near airfields, and
too close to roads, railways and residential areas. Every spot has a **“Check on
dipul.de”** button that takes you straight to the map for the binding check.
