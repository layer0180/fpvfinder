In Germany **every drone** needs liability insurance – however light, with or
without a camera. That goes for a 5-inch freestyle quad as much as for a Tiny
Whoop in the garden.

> As of October 2026. Neither legal nor insurance advice – always read the
> terms of your policy.

## Why insurance is compulsory

German air traffic law treats model aircraft as aircraft too. For them:

- **Strict liability (§ 33 LuftVG):** if your drone causes an accident while
  in operation, you as its keeper are liable for the damage – **even without
  any fault of yours**. A gust, a signal loss, a battery failure: you pay
  anyway.
- **Compulsory insurance (§ 43 LuftVG):** the keeper must carry liability
  insurance for this.
- **Minimum sum (§ 102 LuftVZO):** follows the liability limit in § 37 LuftVG
  – for light aircraft 750,000 units of account (IMF special drawing rights),
  just under one million euros.

Flying without insurance breaches this duty – and in case of damage you are
liable with your own assets.

## Is my private liability insurance enough?

**Often not.** Many private liability policies exclude aircraft or cover
drones only up to a certain mass or only without a camera. Check your terms:

1. Are **drones or model aircraft** expressly included?
2. Up to what **weight**? With a battery, FPV quads quickly exceed 500 g.
3. Does cover apply to drones **with a camera**?
4. Is the **sum insured** at least the legal minimum?
5. Does cover apply in the **open category** without restrictions – also with
   FPV goggles and an observer?
6. Are you insured as the **keeper**, i.e. also when a friend flies your quad?

If the policy is not clear about this, ask in writing.

## The alternatives

- **Dedicated drone liability insurance:** available on its own, often for
  several drones at once, with a confirmation you can show when checked.
- **Membership of a model flying association:** the large associations offer
  their members liability cover for model flying and FPV, and some fly under
  their own operational authorisation.

With any offer, look at the points above – above all the weight limit and
whether FPV is included.

## What the insurance does not cover

- **Damage to your own drone** – there is separate hull cover for that, rarely
  worth it for an affordable FPV quad.
- **Damage caused on purpose.** And deliberately breaking the rules, such as
  flying over crowds, risks trouble with your insurer.
- **Fines.**

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need insurance for a Tiny Whoop under 250 g?** Yes. The duty does not
depend on weight.

**Do I have to carry proof when flying?** You must be able to show that you are
insured. The easiest way is the insurer's confirmation on your phone.

**What is asked at registration?** When you register as an operator with the
Federal Aviation Office (LBA), it asks for your insurance. More in
[Drone licence in Germany: A1/A3 and A2](/en/guides/drone-license-germany-a1-a3-a2).
