**Visitor statistics.** To see how many people use the finder and which links
they come from (a video, say), a self-hosted instance of **Umami** runs on our
own server. Umami sets **no cookies**, stores no IP addresses and builds no
profiles; it counts the page, the referring page, device type, browser, country
and campaign parameters (utm_*) as totals. Nothing is passed to third parties
(Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
