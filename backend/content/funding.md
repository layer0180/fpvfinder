# Support

The finder is free, with no accounts, no ads and no paid tier – and it will stay
that way. Every feature is available to everyone.

Running it publicly does cost a little, and donations go to exactly that:

- **Server** – a small machine holding the finder and the map data for all of
  Germany
- **Domain** – the yearly fee
- **Nothing else** – no salary, no marketing

If the finder got you a good spot and you want to chip in:
**[support it here]({{DONATE_URL}})**. Thank you!

## No strings attached

Donations buy no feature, no priority and no support entitlement. If you would
rather not, just use it – that is what it is for.

## Other ways to help

- **Share a spot** you liked – it helps more than any advertising.
- **Improve OpenStreetMap**: a missing path or nature reserve can be added on
  [openstreetmap.org](https://www.openstreetmap.org), and the finder sees it
  after the next monthly update.
- **Report bugs** at {{PROJECT_URL}}
